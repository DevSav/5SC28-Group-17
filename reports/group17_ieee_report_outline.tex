\begin{abstract}
This report studies model learning and policy learning for the unbalanced disk system.
The system input is the motor voltage and the measured output is the disk angle.
First, Gaussian Process and Artificial Neural Network models are identified from data.
Then, reinforcement learning controllers are used for swing-up and reference tracking.
The final results compare prediction, simulation, and control performance.
\end{abstract}

\begin{IEEEkeywords}
system identification, Gaussian Process, artificial neural network, LSTM, reinforcement learning, Q-learning, unbalanced disk
\end{IEEEkeywords}
